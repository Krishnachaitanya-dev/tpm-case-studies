# Leading a team of 4–5 to ship a virtual jewellery try-on

How I led AISMARTLIVE's main product, Alankara AI, from requirements to release.

| 4–5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|
| people on my team | surfaces: web, tablet and in-store kiosk | product, owned end to end |

- **Company:** AISMARTLIVE Solutions, an early-stage AI startup in Visakhapatnam
- **My role:** Technical Project Lead
- **Timeline:** 2022 to January 2025

## The product

Alankara AI is a computer-vision retail platform for jewellery stores: customers see jewellery on themselves through a camera, using face tracking.

## What I owned

- **Requirements:** what the store needs on the shop floor, and what the customer sees.
- **The try-on itself:** MediaPipe face tracking integrated into the front end, so the jewellery follows the customer's face.
- **Front end and back end:** catalogue, try-on screens and the services behind them.
- **Release on three surfaces:** web, tablets and an in-store kiosk. Each has different screens, cameras and ways of using it.
- **The team:** I led the 4–5 people who built it.

## Other work at AISMARTLIVE

- WhatsApp chatbots for client workflows
- Responsive interfaces, API integrations and technical troubleshooting

## What I'd tell another TPM

A kiosk is a different product from a website, even when the code is shared. Plan the release for each surface separately: the camera, the screen size and the way people stand in front of it all change what "done" means.
