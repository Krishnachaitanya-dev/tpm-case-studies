# One client, two platforms: laundry operations and a training business

How a first project for a business owner led to a second one.

| 2 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|
| platforms for one client | role dashboards: admin, manager, developer | channel customers already use: WhatsApp |

- **Client:** a business owner in Hyderabad running a laundry and ironing service and a building-information-modelling (BIM) training business
- **My role:** Technical Project Manager and developer, from requirements to delivery
- **Timeline:** 2025 to 2026

## Platform 1: a training business (mid-2025)

The first project was a platform for the owner's BIM training business:
- a course catalogue and enrolments
- a student portal and an admin panel
- online class scheduling
- online payments through two payment gateways

## Platform 2: laundry operations on WhatsApp (2025 to 2026)

**The need.** One system for orders, pickups, staff and payments, with WhatsApp as the way customers order.

**What I delivered.** I built the whole system, including the WhatsApp ordering:
- customer registration and ordering on WhatsApp
- pickup time slots and staff assignment
- payment links, UPI QR codes and payment reminders
- dashboards for three roles: admin, manager and developer
- reports for the owner

## Why it matters

The same client came back for a second system about a month after the first project started.

## What I'd tell another TPM

Repeat work from the same client is the clearest proof that a delivery went well. Treat every first project as the sales pitch for the second.
