# Taking a laundry service from idea to both app stores

How I delivered Advance Washing, a doorstep laundry service in Mumbai, working directly with the CEO of The Business Wings.

- **Company:** The Business Wings (Advance Washing is its laundry brand)
- **My role:** Senior Project Manager, owning delivery from requirements to release and support
- **Timeline:** 2025 to now
- **Live on:** [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.advancewashing.app) and the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/advancewashing/id6755901812)

## What had to be delivered

The business needed customers to book a pickup, track the order and pay, and it needed an operations team that could run pickups, orders and support from one place.

Scope, agreed with the CEO and the operations team:

- A customer app for Android and iOS
- Pickup and order flow, from booking to delivery
- An admin console for the operations team
- Support tickets with in-app chat
- Login with a WhatsApp one-time password
- WhatsApp order notifications and push notifications

## How I delivered it

### Store readiness

Getting into the App Store and Google Play was part of the project, not an afterthought:

- Developer-account setup for both stores
- Store review submissions and responses
- Data-safety declarations
- Account deletion inside the app, which both stores require

### Release cycle

I run the release cycle with the CEO. For each change: agree the scope, test it, ship the Android and iOS builds through a CI pipeline, and handle any production fixes after release.

### WhatsApp ordering

In June 2026 I designed and launched ordering over WhatsApp, so customers could book without installing the app. It became the main way customers arrive.

## Results

- Customer app live on Google Play and the App Store
- WhatsApp ordering brings in 80% of registered customers
- Monthly sign-ups grew from single digits to 144 by September 2026
- Company website with pages for six Mumbai service areas

## What I'd tell another TPM

Treat store review and policy compliance as scope from day one. Data-safety forms and in-app account deletion take longer than the feature work if they are left to the end.
